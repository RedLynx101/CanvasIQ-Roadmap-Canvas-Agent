# Design direction

Three directions were considered before implementation: a dark analytics console, a warm editorial strategy studio, and a minimal whiteboard canvas. The studio best supports reading, comparing assumptions and printing decisions without inheriting the old neon treatment. The console overemphasizes instrumentation; a freeform board makes rigorous comparison harder.

Visual thesis: a quiet, warm-paper workspace with graphite type, deep teal actions and precise numeric alignment.

Content plan: open directly to the strategy workspace; orient with project and saved state; place the working table/chart first, contextual assumptions second, and a clear next step last. A compact welcome within a new project replaces the marketing homepage.

Interaction thesis: brief content entrance, a sliding initiative/assistant inspector, and fast selection/focus transitions. Honor reduced motion. Keep the same hierarchy in dark mode and on narrow screens.

The product mark is a simple four-cell canvas with one active cell. Financial charts are functional SVGs with accessible text/table equivalents. No decorative imagery competes with planning data.
